## Day 7

Hi {{ first_name | default: "there" }},

One square this time, because it is the cheapest one on the table to fill.

**The square: llms.txt.** A plain-text file at yourdomain.com/llms.txt that tells an AI assistant, in your words, what your company does, who it is for, what it costs, and where the proof lives. Think of it as the robots.txt of being understood.

**The number.** Of 52 AWS Marketplace sellers I checked, 14 had one. The ones selling AI products were no likelier to have one than the ones selling storage.

**Why it matters.** When the file is there, you get to write the summary of yourself. When it is not, the assistant writes it from whatever your homepage happens to say, superlatives included.

**The move, ten minutes.** Copy mine: [agents.itsrondavis.com/llms.txt](https://agents.itsrondavis.com/llms.txt). Replace my facts with yours. Keep it under a page. Every claim needs a number, a date, a name, or a link. Put the file at the root of your domain and add one line to robots.txt pointing at it.
