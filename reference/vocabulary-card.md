# Vocabulary card — four outcomes, one word

From the Periodic Table of AI Visibility, edition 2026.2. Canonical: https://www.itsrondavis.com/ai-visibility

| Term | Means | Bought by | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ranking** | your position in a list | SEO | position |
| **Citation** | a linked source inside the answer | AEO · GEO | Ci Citations |
| **Mention** | named, no link — the first sign | the same work, earlier | Mt Mentions |
| **Recognition** | the model knows you without looking | LLMO · AIO (training data, slow) | Et Entity · Bs Brand search |
| **AI visibility** | all four | the whole table | Sv Share of voice |

**AEO** leans toward answer boxes (Perplexity, AI Overviews). **GEO** leans toward answers an LLM writes from scratch. In practice they overlap almost completely. **AI SEO** means either "SEO for AI engines" or "using AI to do SEO" — ask which before you agree with anyone.

A local model (Ollama and friends) has only recognition: no live web, so it knows you only if you were in the training data. That is the Entity row's argument, not a surface of its own.

*Listed is ranking. Chosen is the other three.*
