## 2. The eight numbers — unit and direction

Say the unit before the number every time. A number without a unit is the Vague claim tile in your own scoreboard. Cadence: monthly, same prompt set.

| Element | Unit | Direction | Also track |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sv Share of voice** | Percent of prompts in which you are named: named ÷ total prompts × 100. | Higher is better. 20% means named in 6 of 30. | Share of citations (your citations ÷ all citations in the answers), average list position when named (lower is better), or volume-weighted share if some prompts matter more. |
| **Mt Mentions** | Count per period: how many answers named you across the frozen prompt set this month, or mention rate as a percent of prompts. | Higher is better, with one check: a mention can be negative. Tag each one positive, neutral or negative. | Named-but-unlinked vs linked (track separately), mention rate per prompt, or sentiment-split counts. |
| **Ci Citations** | Count per period: answers that link to your domain as a source, or citation rate as cited answers ÷ prompts. | Higher is better. | Unique cited URLs (how many different pages earn citations), citation share (yours ÷ all citations), or position of your link in the source list. |
| **Ps Prompt set** | Not a metric; the ruler. Its unit is a count of prompts (30) and a version date. | Stable is good. Changes between periods should be zero unless deliberate, and every change gets a date. | Coverage: percent of buyer-journey stages or real sales-call questions the set represents. Prompt volume weighting if you have search data. |
| **Sb Switchback** | Lift in percentage points: citation rate in on-weeks minus citation rate in off-weeks. | Higher positive is better. Near zero means the change didn't matter. Negative means undo it. | Ratio (on ÷ off), raw citation count delta. Minimum one week per block and at least two on/off cycles before you believe it. |
| **Ar AI referrals** | Sessions per period from AI referrers (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and the rest), and that segment's conversion rate as a percent. | Higher is better on both. Expect under 2% of sessions; the proof is the conversion rate running several times organic. | Share of total sessions, assisted conversions, revenue per AI session. Many AI visits arrive as 'direct' and are not counted here. |
| **Bs Brand search** | Branded search impressions and clicks per month from Search Console, or a Google Trends index from 0 to 100. | Higher is better, and it lags everything else by weeks to months. | Count of distinct branded queries, direct traffic as a proxy, or brand-name mention volume across the web. |
| **Im Impressions** | Count: times you were shown, as Search Console impressions or an AI-tracking tool's appearances. | Neutral on its own. Read it only as a ratio: citations per 100 impressions. 10,000 impressions and 2 citations is a problem; 500 and 40 is a win. | Impression share within your prompt set, or citations ÷ impressions as the one number worth watching. |

## 4. Switchback test (Sb)

Turning a change on and off in alternating time windows. The only clean test when you cannot split traffic by visitor. Run windows long enough to clear the crawl cycle. A week is usually the floor.
